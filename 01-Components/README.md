# Robot Components

These are the main components we plan to use in our Sumo Robot.

## Main Controller

**Teensy 4.0**

The Teensy is the main controller of the robot.  
It will read the sensors and control the motors.

## Motors

**4 x 32GP Planetary DC Gear Motors**

- Voltage: 12V
- Speed: 160 RPM

We will use four motors to make a 4-wheel-drive robot.

## Motor Drivers

**2 x BTS7960 Motor Drivers**

The motor drivers will control the direction and speed of the motors.

## Opponent Sensors

**5 x TOF200C Distance Sensors**

These sensors will help the robot detect the opponent and know its direction.

## Line Sensors

**IR Line Sensors**

These sensors will be placed under the robot to detect the white edge of the Sumo ring.

## Wheels

**4 x Foam Wheels - 905W-6019**

The wheels will be connected to the four motors.

The final wheel dimensions will be added after we confirm them.

## Wheel Adapters

**6mm Motor Shaft to 12mm Wheel Hex Adapters**

These adapters will connect the motors to the wheels.

## Power System

The robot will use one battery.

The final battery specifications will be added after testing and checking the competition rules.

## Chassis

We will design and build our own chassis.

The motors and wheels will be placed inside the chassis, and the robot will have a front wedge for pushing the opponent.
